`v3` is the recommended configuration rather than the full `v4` pipeline, because the measurement below showed the model stage adds no detections while multiplying wall clock. `v4` is reported in full in the changelog, including that cost.


**How human time is computed.** Every environment a tool flags still reaches a person. A flag carrying an executed exploit costs 2 minutes to confirm, because the reviewer is checking a proof rather than producing one. A flag with no evidence costs a full unaided review, 30 minutes, since the reviewer has to do the whole analysis themselves before they can act. Those two assumptions are the only inputs; disagree with them and you can recompute from `evaluation/results/*.json` directly.

This is where the baseline's reviewer cost comes from. It flags 2 of 15 environments and attaches no executed evidence to any of them, so each flag costs a full unaided review and each of the 7 defects it missed reaches a lab unexamined. `v3` flags 8 and attaches a runnable exploit to 8 of them, which is why the same corpus costs 60 reviewer-minutes on the baseline and 16 here.
